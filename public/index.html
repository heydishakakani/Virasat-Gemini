Questions you might have

Is my family's financial data sent anywhere?+

Virasat never submits what you enter to a bank, registrar, insurer, or government body. The one exception is the optional “Ask Virasat” box: when you use it, your question and the names of your listed assets (never account numbers) are sent to Google’s Gemini to write an answer.

What kinds of assets can I add?+

Bank accounts, mutual funds, insurance policies, property, and other holdings — anything a family member owned that now needs a nominee check.

What does a "nominee check" actually tell me?+

For each asset, Virasat gives a plain yes, no, or unknown for whether a nominee is on file, so you immediately see what's straightforward and what needs follow-up paperwork.

How long does it take to set up?+

Most families list their first few assets in under ten minutes. You can add more as you find them — there's no need to have everything at once.

Do I need an account to try it?+

You can start an asset review right away. Creating an account just lets you save your progress and come back to it later.
